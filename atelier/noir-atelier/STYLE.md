# Noir Atelier — Style Prompt

> A dark room, one shaft of light, and a single thread of gold that leads the eye from shot to shot. The object is revealed by what the light is allowed to touch.
> References (grammar only): chiaroscuro still life; fragrance and jewellery films shot on black; lacquer boxes; a tailor's workroom after hours. Never copy a brand's film, bottle, mark or typeface.

Technique base: `glass-product` (lighting, reflections, depth of field), `art-deco` (the gold line and its sheen). Atelier style: no demo film yet, see [`../README.md`](../README.md).

## 1. Essence, and what it is not

Darkness is the ground and the subject is carved out of it by one hard-edged light. A fine gold thread, one or two pixels wide, crosses every shot: it draws an outline, becomes a seam, turns into a rule under the title. It is the only continuous thing in the film, and the cut follows it.

Not a neon film (no glow, no colour cast), not a product spin on a turntable, not film noir pastiche (no blinds, no smoke, no detective).

## 2. Materials and rendering

- **Ground**: warm black `#0b0a09`, with a faint falloff toward one corner so it never reads as a void. Fine grain at 3 to 4 %.
- **Lacquer and glass**: deep, glossy, almost black; they are seen only by their reflections. One long soft-box reflection per surface, with a hard edge on one side.
- **The thread**: a banded gold gradient (dark, light, hot, light, dark) with a hairline highlight. It has no glow. It is drawn progressively along its path, never faded in whole.
- **Light**: one key, a narrow shaft with a visible edge, 30 to 45° above the subject. No fill. Dust in the beam is allowed, sparse and slow.
- **Depth of field**: shallow. Out-of-focus highlights become small warm discs.

## 3. Colour logic

Black, gold, and one ivory for skin, paper or type. One deep accent may belong to the subject (oxblood, bottle green or midnight blue), seen only where the light lands. Nothing else is coloured.

## 4. Type

A light high-contrast serif (Cormorant Garamond Light), in ivory, small, tracked wide in capitals for labels and set large in lowercase italic for the one title. The thread draws a rule under the title, then the title fades up.

## 5. Motion and camera

- The light moves more than the camera. A reveal is the shaft sliding across the object over 3 to 4 s.
- Camera: slow push-in or a slow lateral track, never both. No handheld, no rotation around the object.
- Cuts land when the thread reaches the edge of frame; the next shot picks it up at the opposite edge.
- Shots of 4 to 6 s. The last image holds for 3 s.

## 6. Sound

Solo piano in a low register or a bowed string, with long rests. A room tone you can almost hear. Foley close and dry: a lid, a ribbon, a glass stopper. One soft low note on the main reveal. No percussion.

## 7. Native moves

- **The thread draws the object** before the light finds it: an outline in gold on black, then the shaft arrives and the outline is true.
- **The seam**: the thread runs down the frame, and the picture parts along it into the next shot.
- **Light as a page turn**: the shaft sweeps out of frame and returns on a different object.
- **The rule**: the thread comes to rest as a short horizontal line; the title sits on it.

## 8. Pitfalls

- More than one light. A second light makes it a catalogue photograph.
- A thread that glows, pulses or doubles. It is a drawn line.
- Lifting the blacks so the whole object is visible. What is not lit stays unknown.
- Gold type, gold thread and a gold object together. If the object is gold, the type is ivory.
